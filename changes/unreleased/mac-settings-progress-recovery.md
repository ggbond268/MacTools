Mac Settings shows live apply and restore progress, supports retrying unfinished profile changes, and keeps favorites synchronized with operation state.

Incomplete restorations retain local recovery snapshots with Retry Restoration and Keep Current Values choices. New edits wait until recovery is resolved.

Unreleased development history starts fresh without migrating old snapshots; saved profiles, favorites, and macOS values are unchanged.
